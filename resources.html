Find reproductive health care

Looking for support?

If you're experiencing severe period pain, irregular periods, heavy bleeding, pelvic pain, or think you may have a reproductive health condition such as endometriosis, PMOS (formerly PCOS), adenomyosis, PMDD, or fibroids, you deserve to be heard.

These hospitals and clinics provide specialized care for adolescents and young adults with reproductive health concerns.

Women's College Hospital+
Specialized hospital One of Canada's leading women's health hospitals.
  • Young Women's Gynecology Clinic (ages 17–25)
  • Endometriosis care
  • PMOS/PCOS management
  • Pelvic pain
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Fibroids
  • Ovarian cysts
  • Menstrual suppression
  • Birth control counselling

A physician referral is required for most specialty clinics.

The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), Pediatric & Adolescent Gynecology+
Specialized hospital Designed specifically for children and adolescents.
  • Severe menstrual pain
  • Pelvic pain
  • Endometriosis
  • Menstrual disorders
  • Ovarian cysts
  • Puberty concerns
  • Reproductive endocrine disorders
  • Adolescent sexual health

A multidisciplinary team includes gynecologists, nurses, dietitians, and social workers.

St. Michael's Hospital Gynecology Clinic+
Specialized hospital Provides adolescent gynecology and pelvic health services.
  • Pelvic pain
  • Menstrual disorders
  • General gynecology
  • Social work support
  • Specialized pelvic health clinics

Some physicians here have specialized training or clinical interests in minimally invasive gynecologic surgery and endometriosis.

Planned Parenthood Toronto+
Youth-friendly sexual & reproductive health clinic Provides confidential youth-friendly care.
  • Birth control counselling
  • STI testing
  • Menstrual health support
  • Pregnancy testing
  • Referrals to gynecologists
  • Sexual health education

Some services are available even without a health card.

Advanced Pelvic Physiotherapy Centre+
Pelvic pain & pelvic floor therapy For some people living with endometriosis, adenomyosis, or chronic pelvic pain, pelvic floor physiotherapy can be an important part of treatment. This clinic provides specialized pelvic floor assessment and rehabilitation for chronic pelvic pain, endometriosis-related symptoms, bladder concerns, and other pelvic health conditions.
Proactive Pelvic Health Centre+
Pelvic pain & pelvic floor therapy Specialized pelvic floor assessment and rehabilitation for chronic pelvic pain, endometriosis-related symptoms, bladder concerns, and other pelvic health conditions.
ⓘ A note for students. If you feel your pain or concerns are being dismissed, it's okay to seek a second opinion. Severe period pain, ongoing pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, or symptoms that interfere with school, sports, sleep, or daily life deserve medical attention. You know your body best, and your concerns should be taken seriously.

Clinic programs and referral requirements change. Call ahead or check the hospital's own website before you book.

Emergency

If pain is sudden, severe, and unlike anything before, or you're fainting, vomiting, or bleeding through more than a pad an hour, treat it as an emergency.
